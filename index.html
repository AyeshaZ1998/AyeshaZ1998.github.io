About me

The combination of my background, academics, and work experience has uniquely shaped not only who I am but the career I want to pursue. As a Pakistani citizen who grew up as a third-culture child in the UAE, I have seen firsthand what development really looks like on the ground. I know better than most the distance between decisions made in faraway offices and the lives they quietly reshape. Seeing that disconnect up close is what pushed me to enter the international development sector. My focus on social equality and human rights was driven further by the injustices I encountered along the way, the privileges afforded to those from the Global North, the added difficulties our identities carry, and the roadblocks I faced as a South Asian woman.

In my career so far, I've developed three core strengths: the quantitative skills to analyze problems, the theoretical foundation to understand them, and the ground-level perspective to know what they mean for real people. At the heart of my work is a simple philosophy: to stay close to the ground, rooted in fieldwork and genuine connection with communities, and to use my own positionality to give voice to those who don't share my privilege.
