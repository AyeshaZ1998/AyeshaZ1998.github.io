COVID-19 Policy Stringency Analysis (R)

R · tidyverse · Data visualization · Regression

An independent analysis examining how government policy responses related to COVID-19 cases and deaths across countries and income groups. The project merges Oxford's COVID policy and stringency data, Our World in Data mortality figures, and World Bank income classifications into a single cleaned dataset, then builds choropleth world maps of stringency and income levels, generates distribution charts across income groups, runs correlation and multicollinearity checks, and fits lagged regression models linking policy measures to case and death outcomes, complete with coefficient plots.

R · tidyverse · Data cleaning & merging · ggplot2 · Choropleth mapping · Correlation analysis · Lagged regression · Reproducible R Markdown

Southeast Asian Deportation Analysis (R)

R · Administrative data · Poisson regression

The full code behind my MPP capstone, analyzing detention and deportation patterns among Southeast Asian nationals using four large administrative datasets. The repository covers data cleaning and merging across sources, population-at-risk estimation, and Poisson regression modeling of enforcement trends.

R · Large administrative datasets · Data cleaning & merging · Poisson regression · Data visualization · Reproducible workflows

Cross-Country Study on Culture & Economic Incentives (R)

R · Panel data · Fixed-effects regression

The code behind my independent study building a harmonized cross-country panel of 156 countries over two decades. The repository covers data consolidation from multiple global datasets, missing-data handling, and fixed-effects and robust regressions with clustered standard errors, all scripted as a reproducible R Markdown workflow.

R · R Markdown · Panel data · Fixed-effects regression · Clustered standard errors · Data harmonization · Data visualization

Flood Vulnerability Index for Sindh, Pakistan

ArcGIS StoryMap · Spatial analysis

A spatial framework for disaster management that maps flood risk across Sindh by combining climate exposure, hazard, and social vulnerability into a single index, highlighting where communities are least equipped to withstand intensifying climate events.

Racial Disparities in Maternal Health, California

ArcGIS StoryMap · Spatial analysis

A spatial analysis of maternal health outcomes across California, using maternal mortality as a proxy to surface the racial disparities in care and the systemic factors shaping women's survival before, during, and after childbirth.
