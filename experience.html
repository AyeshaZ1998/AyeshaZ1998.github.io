Jan 2026 – May 2026

CCDE Fellow, Research Consultant

AAPI Force · Berkeley, USA

Report and code available in the Portfolio section.

For this fellowship, I investigated who the United States is actually deporting from the Southeast Asian refugee community: Cambodian, Laotian, and Vietnamese nationals whose presence in the U.S. is itself a consequence of decades of American military engagement in the region. Drawing on four large administrative datasets covering ICE arrests, detentions, removals, and immigration court proceedings, supplemented by interviews with directly impacted individuals and advocacy organizations, I documented an unprecedented intensification of enforcement against this community under the current administration. I found that removals reached a 17-fold increase over the prior baseline, that 41 percent of executed orders were at least fifteen years old, and that the population being removed bears little resemblance to the "worst of the worst" rhetoric used to justify it. The report pairs quantitative analysis with the lived histories behind the numbers, and closes with policy recommendations at the state, federal, and structural levels.

Data analysis · R · Large administrative datasets · Poisson regression · Data cleaning & merging · Data visualization · Qualitative interviews · Policy analysis · Legal & immigration policy · Report writing · Immigration & refugee policy

Jun 2025 – Sep 2025

WILDLABS Intern

World Wildlife Fund · Washington, DC

Read the State of Conservation Technology report

I spent the summer at WWF's WILDLABS, studying the state of technology in global conservation. I analyzed five years of survey data spanning eleven conservation technologies in R, and complemented the quantitative work with qualitative coding in NVivo to surface the patterns behind the numbers: what tools practitioners were adopting, what was holding them back, and where the field was heading. I conducted interviews across the organization, including with WWF's President and CEO, and co-authored the State of Conservation Technology report that came out of the work.

I had not worked in conservation before, and the experience made me realize how connected everything is, and how much I want to be a generalist who can move across fields. It also let me combine my quantitative and qualitative skills in one project, and showed me that the work I care about can be done in many different forms.

R · Data analysis · Survey analysis · NVivo · Qualitative coding · Mixed-methods research · Stakeholder interviews · Report writing · Conservation technology · Research synthesis
